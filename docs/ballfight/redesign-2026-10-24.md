# BALLFIGHT v2 redesign (user, Oct 24; approved) — attacks + effects for #29–#35
**Direction (user, Oct 24):** "I would like the instruments' attacks to be even more unique from each other, as well as
have more visual character. A few of the instruments all just shoot projectiles … it feels uninspired because they all
shoot in relatively the same way. Attacks like the Harp, Organ, and Trombone feel genuinely unique and are creatively
representative of the instrument. The Piano, Guitar, Drums, and Violin can keep their attacks (but some visual
improvement would still be good) since I already uploaded those videos. Most other instruments should be revisited."
On effects: "I am okay with some more intense visual effects. The only visual effect I think is off limits is a
full-screen flash."
**Design rule:** the instrument's own body, or the way it's played, is the weapon. No two fighters share a kind of
attack. Every attack lands on the beat grid; every landed hit grows the signature (as before). Hooks, captions rules,
colours, HUD, count-in, mood, tempo and loop rules of each episode stay as they are.

**Kept (mechanics unchanged; effects upgraded):** GUITAR spinning neck blade · PIANO key volleys · DRUMS beat pulse ·
VIOLIN fast bow · TROMBONE slide lance (SAD TROMBONE, POWER SLIDE, PEDAL TONE) · ORGAN floor eruptions · HARP tripwire
strings. Guitar / Piano / Drums / Violin: their uploaded videos are not re-rendered; the effect upgrades are for future
crossovers and must not change their fights (#27 seed 88 and #28 seed 568 replay identically).

    Translucent copper **echo ghosts** trail behind along the horn's path (where it was 1, 2, 3, 4 beats ago), and each
    ghost repeats the blast one beat after the one before, like a horn call echoing across a valley. **+1 echo ghost
    per landed hit (max 4).** The attack is literally a delay effect: the call plus quieter, darker delayed repeats.
  - **LV2 STOPPED HORN** (`shield`: a copper hand plugs the bell; no hounds any more) · **LV3 HORN RIP** (the echoes
    come back faster: half-beat spacing, with a rip up the harmonic series) · **below 25 HP FINAL FLICK** (a giant white
    glove bursts out of the bell, winds up for a beat and flicks the enemy across the ring; extra damage if it hits the
    ropes within ~0.9 s, like the tuba's spit).

  - New engine kinds (additive, own commits): `echo` weapon (position history + delayed repeats), `flick` ability.

**Shared effects kit (build once, in `reelkit/fx.py`, small separate commits; the #35 session builds it first):**
- **Attacks light the scene:** every attack and impact is a local light in its fighter's colour on the ring floor,
  ropes and crowd.
- **Impacts:** a 1–2 frame impact burst at the hit point (ink-starburst style, local); a little camera shake on heavy
  hits; heat-shimmer distortion ripples on shockwaves and blasts; chromatic fringing on big hits (local).
- **Particles with trails**, specific to each instrument: steam (trumpet), smoke (sax), tartan shreds (bagpipes), air
  puffs (accordion), vinyl shards (turntables), sparks + glitch blocks (synth), wind streaks (tuba), scales / glass
  dust (flute), sea foam (ukulele), wood chips (acoustic), copper embers (horn), dust (organ), shimmer motes (harp),
  brass sparks (trombone); for future crossovers: guitar swing smear + sparks, piano key shards, drumhead ripple
  rings, violin rosin-dust ribbon.
- **Marks that fade on the ring canvas:** scorches, claw marks, cracks, water, smoke rings.
- **Ability moments:** the background dims slightly (<= 30 %, eased) and speed lines stream behind the fighter as the
  ability name punches in.
- **Off limits: full-screen flashes.** Bright bursts stay under about a quarter of the frame and at most 3 per second;
  `reelkit.flashcheck` must pass.
