# Engine 2, milestone E4 (director and time) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Build `bf2-direct`, which holds three things:
- the **time map**: slow-mo ramps into specials and the K.O. cinematic, with fight time and video time as one
  monotonic map that picture, effects, sound and HUD all read;
- the **camera director**: framing in the safe zone, punch-ins, tilts, whip-backs, launch follows, low angles on
  slams, shake, the K.O. shot, and spacing so the minute breathes;
- the **arena reactions**: rope wobble, crowd jumps, LED flashes, beat light.

Then wire all three into the Engine 2 render job and into the seed fit check.

**Architecture:** everything is precomputed once per fight from the `Recording2`, so any frame can be drawn in any
order:
- `TimeMap::build(rec, cfg)` uses only the events and the step count, so it's cheap enough for the seed search;
- `CameraTrack::build(rec, &map, cfg)` simulates a smoothed camera over all 1,830 frames in order, deterministically,
  and stores one `Camera` per frame;
- `ArenaTrack::build` does the same for the arena's reaction values.

`bf2-render` reads them through `FrameCtx`. Hitstop is already in the fight (bodies freeze while fight time runs),
so the time map adds only slow-mo and the K.O. freeze.

**Tech Stack:** Rust, `bf2-sim` (events v2, `Recording2`), `bf2-render` (`Camera`, `Scene`, `stage`), `bf2-fx`
(`FxTimeline`, the `step_to_t` hook), `bf-core` (`grid`, `dmath`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-engine2-design.md` section 4, section 2 "Fit to the video", and
section 8 row E4.

## Global Constraints

- The video is 61.0 s at 30 fps (1,830 frames). The fight starts at `t_fight` (from `Grid`) in video time. After
  the last recorded step, the picture holds that step.
- Safe zone: today's `SAFE = (20, 96, 340, 500)` in 360 x 640 internal pixels (`bf-picture/src/config.rs`). At 1080
  x 1920 that's `(60, 288, 1020, 1500)`. Both balls' silhouettes stay inside it for at least 98 % of fight frames,
  and inside the frame always.
- Determinism: the director is pure CPU on `dmath`, and its noise is a hash of frame and seed. The same recording
  gives the same tracks.
- Search fit: the K.O. window is measured in **video time** after slow-mo. The time map must build in under 50 µs
  from events alone, so the search can use it on every seed.
- No flash cap. Music slows with slow-mo by default (E5 does the audio side, and E4 only exposes `rate`).
- Test only `bf2-direct`, the changed parts of `bf2-render` and `bf-export` job2, and `bf-engine`.

## Rulings carried in

- **Time map shape.** The map is a list of fight-time segments, each with a constant `speed`, meaning fight seconds
  per video second (1 is normal, 0.25 is 4x slow, 0 is a freeze of a set video length). Ramps are 6 linear
  sub-segments, which is smooth enough at 30 fps and keeps the map exactly invertible. Total added video time is
  capped (`DirectCfg::max_extra = 4.0 s`). When cues would exceed the cap, the later, weaker slow-mos are dropped
  first.
- **Freeze at K.O.:** 0.35 s of video (today's `HITSTOP`), then 0.3x for 0.8 s of fight time, then normal.

## Review Focus

1. **Monotonic and invertible.** `video_t(fight_t(v)) ≈ v` and `fight_t(video_t(f)) ≈ f` everywhere, including
   inside freezes, where `fight_t` is constant and `video_t` of that fight time returns the freeze's start. Pinned
   in Task 1 (`round_trips_everywhere`).
2. **Overlapping slow-mo cues** (a special during a K.O., two specials close together) must merge, never stack
   below the slowest speed or exceed the cap. Pinned in Task 1 (`overlapping_cues_merge`,
   `extra_time_is_capped`).
3. **A fight with no K.O.** No K.O. cinematic, and the map is identity apart from cues. Pinned in Task 1
   (`no_ko_no_freeze`).
4. **Both balls far apart or flying high.** A launch to the top of an arc while the other ball is in a far corner
   must still be framed. Pinned in Task 3 (`launch_corner_is_framed`).
5. **Shot spam.** Twenty specials in ten seconds must not give twenty punch-ins. Pinned in Task 3
   (`big_shots_keep_their_spacing`).

### Task 1: The time map

**Files:** Modify `studio/crates/bf2-direct/Cargo.toml` (deps `bf2-sim`, `bf-core`). Create
`studio/crates/bf2-direct/src/{lib.rs,time.rs,cfg.rs}`.

**Interfaces:**
- Produces: `pub struct DirectCfg { pub max_extra: f64 /*4.0*/, pub ramp_in: f64 /*0.15 fight s*/, pub hold_after:
  f64 /*0.25*/, pub ramp_out: f64 /*0.2*/, pub ko_freeze: f64 /*0.35 video s*/, pub ko_slow: f64 /*0.3*/, pub
  ko_slow_len: f64 /*0.8 fight s*/, pub big_shot_gap: f64 /*2.5 video s*/, pub slowmo: bool /*true; false turns
  every slow-mo off, for tests and a later switch*/ }` with `Default`.
- Produces: `pub struct TimeMap { /*segments*/ }`:
  - `pub fn build(rec_events: &[bf2_sim::Ev], steps: u32, dt: f64, t_fight: f64, ko: Option<u32>, cfg:
    &DirectCfg) -> TimeMap`;
  - `pub fn from_recording(rec: &Recording2, cfg) -> TimeMap` (a convenience wrapper);
  - `fn video_t(&self, fight_t: f64) -> f64`;
  - `fn fight_t(&self, video_t: f64) -> f64`;
  - `fn rate(&self, video_t: f64) -> f64` (fight seconds per video second, 0 during a freeze);
  - `fn step_video_t(&self, step: u32) -> f64`;
  - `fn ko_video_t(&self) -> Option<f64>`;
  - `fn extra(&self) -> f64` (total added video seconds).

  Fight time here is measured from the fight start, and video time is absolute (`t_fight` included).
- Slow-mo comes from `EvKind::Cue(Cue::SlowMo { scale })`. It ramps in over `ramp_in` fight seconds before the
  cue's step, which is fine because the map is built after the fight, so it can lead into the moment. It holds
  `scale` until the move's next `Recovery` event for the same fighter plus `hold_after`, then ramps out.

### Task 3: The camera director

**Files:** Create `studio/crates/bf2-direct/src/{camera.rs,shots.rs,project.rs}`.

**Interfaces:**
- Produces: `#[derive(Clone, Copy)] pub struct CamPose { pub eye: [f64; 3] /*render space*/, pub target: [f64; 3],
  pub roll: f64, pub fov_deg: f64 }`. `bf2-render` gets `impl From<CamPose> for Camera`.
- Produces: `pub fn project(c: &CamPose, p_render: [f64; 3], w: f64, h: f64) -> Option<(f64, f64)>` (a CPU
  projection matching the renderer's, used by the framing logic and the tests). The render-space convention is
  E2's `to_render`, re-implemented here as `pub fn to_render(v) -> [f64; 3]`, with a test that it matches
  `bf2_render::to_render`.
- Produces: `pub struct CameraTrack { pub poses: Vec<CamPose> /*1,830*/, pub shake: Vec<[f64; 2]> /*screen-space
  offset px*/ }` and `CameraTrack::build(rec, map, cfg) -> CameraTrack`. The algorithm, per frame in order:
  1. **Base shot:** the target is the two balls' midpoint, lifted by the higher ball's z × 0.6. The distance comes
     from their separation plus height, clamped from 14 to 30, and is the smallest that keeps both balls' circles
     inside the safe zone with a 6 % margin (found by bisection with `project`). Pitch is 58°, fov 36°, yaw 0.
  2. **Shot requests** from events, each with a strength and a priority (Special > Heavy > others):
     - `PunchIn` at Windup: distance × 0.62 toward the attacker, over the wind-up;
     - `Tilt(a)`: roll a°;
     - `WhipBack`: return to base in 0.12 s with a 6 % overshoot;
     - `Launch`: follow z, pitch −8°;
     - `Slam`: low angle, pitch −14° for 0.6 s;
     - `Clash`: a quick 0.9x push-in;
     - `Ko`: push-in on the loser, a slow 12° orbit across the freeze and slow, then a pull-out to the wide winner
       shot by K.O. plus 2.5 s.

     Big shots (PunchIn, Slam, the K.O. shot) are refused within `big_shot_gap` of the last accepted big shot,
     unless their priority is higher.
  3. **Smoothing:** a critically damped spring on target, distance, roll and pitch. Its half-life is 0.18 s
     normally and 0.05 s inside a whip.
  4. **Framing guard:** after the shot is applied, if a ball leaves the safe zone, the distance is raised until it
     fits again (a shot never breaks framing).
  5. **Shake:** trauma from `Shake(x)` cues and from contacts at strength ≥ 0.4 (strength²), decaying 1.6 per
     second. The offset is trauma² × 18 px × a 2D hash-noise of video time at 25 Hz. The roll shake is trauma² ×
     1.5°.

### Task 4: Arena reactions

**Files:** Create `studio/crates/bf2-direct/src/arena.rs`. Modify `studio/crates/bf2-render/src/stage.rs` to read
it.

**Interfaces:**
- Produces: `pub struct ArenaFrame { pub rope_wobble: [f64; 4] /*per side, signed displacement*/, pub crowd_jump:
  f64 /*0..1*/, pub led_flash: Option<(usize /*fighter*/, f64 /*0..1*/)>, pub leader: Option<usize>, pub
  beat: f64 /*0..1 pulse on the grid's beat*/, pub house: f64 /*house lights 0..1; dips on specials*/ }`.
- Produces: `pub struct ArenaTrack { pub frames: Vec<ArenaFrame> }` and `ArenaTrack::build(rec, map, bpm: f64,
  cfg) -> ArenaTrack`. The pieces:
  - `Bounce{Rope}` starts a damped oscillation on the nearest side (4 Hz, decay 3/s, amplitude from speed);
  - contacts at strength ≥ 0.6 and the K.O. make the crowd jump (rise 0.08 s, fall 0.5 s);
  - the LEDs flash in the attacker's colour on heavy and special contacts;
  - the leader is whoever has more HP;
  - the beat comes from the bpm grid;
  - house lights fall to 0.35 on `Special` Windup and recover after Recovery.

  `Stage::draw` uses them: rope cylinders bent by the wobble, crowd instances raised by jump × 0.25, the LED
  colour and flash, ambient × house, and a beat pulse on the LED boards.
