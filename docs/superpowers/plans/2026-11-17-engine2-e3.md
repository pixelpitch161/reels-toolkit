# Engine 2, milestone E3 (effects toolkit) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Build `bf2-fx`, the shared effects toolkit Engine 2 fighters build their signature looks from:
- particles on the GPU (100k+ live);
- trails and smears on weapon bones;
- mesh effects (shock rings, domes, beams);
- decals on the canvas and ropes;
- emissive effect lights;
- screen effects: motion blur, radial blur, shockwave distortion, bloom with lens dirt, chromatic aberration,
  speed lines, full-screen flash and invert, letterbox, vignette, grain, grade;
- hit presets that scale with `strength`.

Every effect has an exit. Then render the TEST DUMMY fight with all of it for Aedan's review.

**Architecture:** effects are **stateless in time**. Each effect is a record with a start time, a lifetime, an exit
kind and parameters. The shaders compute an effect's look at any video time from that record alone. For
particles, each one's state is a closed-form function of its emitter, its index and its age (ballistic plus
analytic drag, plus hash noise, plus a floor bounce). Any frame can therefore be drawn in any order, the same on
every draw, which is what the preview's scrubbing, the multi-pass render and determinism need. There is no compute
state to carry between frames.

On the CPU, `FxTimeline` is built once per fight from the recording's events. The presets and the looks add their
own records. Each frame, the active records are culled and uploaded. `bf2-render` gets two hook points: an
`fx_pass` (HDR, after glass) and a `post_chain` (HDR, before tone mapping).

**Tech Stack:** `bf2-render` (E2), wgpu 30.0.1, WGSL, `bf2-sim` events v2.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-engine2-design.md` section 3, "Effects toolkit", plus anti-goals
(no cheap or small effects, no abrupt exits) and section 8 row E3.

## Global Constraints

- **Exits are mandatory.** Every `FxRecord` has `life > 0` and an `Exit` other than none. `Exit::Cut` doesn't
  exist. A summoned mesh ends in `Dissolve`, `Burst`, `Shrink` or `FlyOff`. A test enumerates every preset and
  every look-made record.
- **No flash cap** (Aedan, 2026-11-17 05:25Z). Full-screen flashes and inverts are allowed.
- **Determinism:** effect randomness is a hash of `(record seed, particle index)` in WGSL (PCG hash), never a
  runtime RNG. Record seeds come from the event's step and its index in the event list.
- **Budgets on the 9070 XT** (checked at the PC step): at 150k live particles plus all post effects, a frame
  takes under 16 ms of GPU time (sub-frame motion blur multiplies only the scene passes).
- Quality bar: big and dense, never cheap. Particles are soft and additive with HDR colour, so they bloom.
  Light hits and heavy hits must read very differently.
- Never edit `projects/`, `reelkit/`, `series/`. TEST DUMMY stays hidden. Test only `bf2-fx`, `bf2-render`'s
  changed parts and `bf-content` `fighters2`.

## Rulings carried in

- **Motion blur is sub-frame accumulation**, not a velocity buffer. The scene is rendered at `n` sub-frame times
  across the shutter (180°, so half a frame) and averaged in HDR. `n` is 1 when nothing moves fast, and up to 8
  when a body or bone tip moves more than 0.4 ring units per frame, or a cue asks. It's exact for rigs and
  particles, with no velocity artefacts, at GPU cost only. Velocity-buffer blur can be added later if speed needs
  it.
- **Time is video time** until E4's time map exists: `t_video = t_fight + step·dt`. `FxTimeline` takes a
  `fn(step) -> t_video` so E4 can swap in the map without touching effects.

## Review Focus

1. **An effect that pops out.** At the last frame of every record's life, its contribution must already be near 0
   (under 2 % of its peak luminance), whatever its exit. Pinned in Task 7
   (`every_record_fades_to_nothing_by_its_end`).
2. **Particle explosions at huge counts.** 200k particles in one burst must render without a buffer overflow or a
   driver timeout, and anything over budget is dropped oldest-first with a log line. Pinned in Task 2
   (`over_budget_drops_oldest`).
3. **Effects through a K.O. and past the fight's end.** Records that start late keep their full exit even after
   the last step. Pinned in Task 7 (`late_records_finish_after_the_fight`).
4. **Scrubbing.** Frame `f` rendered after frame `f+300` equals frame `f` rendered first. Pinned in Task 2
   (`particles_are_stateless`) and Task 6 (`post_chain_is_stateless`).
5. **Screen effects stacking.** A flash, a radial blur, a shockwave and chromatic aberration on the same frame
   must not blow out to NaN or infinity, or go solid white for longer than the flash itself. Pinned in Task 6
   (`stacked_screen_effects_stay_finite`).

### Task 2: Stateless GPU particles

**Files:** Create `studio/crates/bf2-fx/src/particles.rs`, `src/shaders/particles.wgsl`. Modify `bf2-render` to
expose the `fx_pass` hook: `Renderer::render_with(gpu, scene, post, fx: &mut dyn FxPass)`, where `trait FxPass {
fn draw(&mut self, ctx: &mut FxCtx) }` gives the HDR target, depth (read-only), the camera uniforms and the
encoder.

**Interfaces:**
- Produces: `pub struct BurstDef { pub count: u32, pub speed: (f32, f32), pub spread: f32 /*cone half-angle rad;
  π = sphere*/, pub dir: [f32; 3], pub gravity: f32, pub drag: f32, pub size: (f32, f32), pub colour: [f32; 3]
  /*HDR*/, pub colour_end: [f32; 3], pub stretch: f32 /*velocity streak*/, pub bounce: f32 /*0 = dies on floor*/,
  pub shape: Shape /*Spark, Ember, Smoke, Debris, Note*/, pub emit_over: f32 /*seconds; 0 = instant*/ }`.
- Produces: `pub struct Particles` with `new(gpu, r: &Renderer)` and `fn draw(&mut self, ctx, recs: &[(&FxRecord,
  [f64; 3] /*resolved anchor*/)], t: f64)`. One instanced draw per shape. The vertex shader computes particle `i`:
  - its birth `tb = emit_over · h(i)`;
  - its age `a = t − t0 − tb`, skipped if negative or at least the particle's life;
  - its velocity from the cone and speed range using `h(i, 1..3)`;
  - its position from analytic drag `p = p0 + v·(1 − e^(−drag·a))/drag + ½g·a²`;
  - a floor bounce, solved analytically for the first bounce only;
  - its size and colour lerped over its life.

  The fragment shader draws a soft disc, a streak along screen velocity, or a smoke billow, all additive HDR.
  Smoke uses premultiplied over-blending, sorted coarsely by record.
- Produces: `pub const MAX_LIVE: u32 = 262_144`. Over budget, whole records are dropped oldest-first and a counter
  is exposed (`dropped()`).

- [ ] **Step 1: Tests** at 270 x 480 on lavapipe:
  - `burst_is_dense_and_bright` (a 20k spark burst at the ring centre lights more than 5 % of pixels above 0.8
    luminance at its peak);
  - `particles_are_stateless` (frame t then t+1 s then t equals t, byte for byte);
  - `floor_bounce_stays_above_floor` (CPU reference of the same closed form: z ≥ 0 for every particle over life);
  - `over_budget_drops_oldest` (300k requested: `dropped() > 0`, and the newest record still draws);
  - `exit_fades_particles` (the final frame of life is below 2 % of peak).
- [ ] **Step 2: Run them to see them fail.**
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement.** The WGSL closed form must match a CPU reference function in `particles.rs`, used by the

### Task 6: The screen effect chain and motion blur

**Files:** Create `studio/crates/bf2-fx/src/screen.rs`, `src/shaders/{bloom,radial,shock,chroma,lines,flash,grade}.wgsl`.
Modify `bf2-render`: add `post_chain` and `Renderer::render_accum(gpu, scenes: &[Scene], weights: &[f32], post,

**Interfaces:**
- Produces: `pub struct ScreenDef { pub effect: ScreenFx, pub amount: f32 }`, with these effects:
  - `ScreenFx::Flash { colour }` and `Invert`;
  - `RadialBlur { centre: Anchor }` (16 taps toward the point);
  - `Shockwave { centre: Anchor, speed: f32 }` (a screen-space refraction ring);
  - `Chroma { centre: Anchor }`;
  - `SpeedLines { centre: Anchor, colour }`;
  - `Letterbox`;
  - `Shake` (a camera offset, applied to the camera, not the image);
  - `Grade { lift, gamma, gain, saturation }`;
  - `Desaturate { keep: [f32; 3] /*keep hues near this*/ }`.
- Produces: the fixed chain order, run each frame in HDR:
  1. shockwave refraction;
  2. radial blur;
  3. bloom (a dual-filter 6-level pyramid, threshold 1.0, with procedural lens dirt);
  4. chroma;
  5. speed lines;
  6. flash and invert;
  7. grade and desaturate;
  8. vignette;
  9. grain (hashed, `seed = frame`);
  10. letterbox;
  11. tone map (E2's).
- Produces: `pub fn subframes(rec, t: f64, timeline) -> u32` (1 to 8, per the ruling) and `pub fn shutter_times(t,
  n) -> Vec<f64>` (180° shutter centred on the frame).

### Task 7: Presets, event wiring and the exit audit

**Files:** Create `studio/crates/bf2-fx/src/presets.rs`, `src/wire.rs`.

**Interfaces:**
- Produces: `pub fn hit_light(at, normal, colour, strength) -> Vec<FxRecord>`, plus `hit_heavy`,
  `special_impact`, `launch_trail`, `land_dust`, `slam_crater`, `clash_spark`, `block_flare`, `ko_burst`,
  `proj_spawn_flash` and `proj_expire_puff`. Each scales count, size, light, shake, bloom push and screen effects
  with `strength`:
  - **light** (under 0.4): about 600 sparks, a small ring, a short light, shake 0.15, no screen effects;
  - **heavy** (0.4 to 0.85): about 6k sparks plus 1.5k embers, a ring with distort, a decal crack, shake 0.5,
    radial blur 0.3, a light;
  - **special** (above 0.85 or Tag::Special): about 40k sparks, embers and smoke, a dome, two rings, a scorch,
    flash 0.8, shockwave, radial blur 0.7, chroma, speed lines, shake 1.0, a long light;
  - **ko**: about 120k shards and embers, a big flash, invert 2 frames, desaturate keeping the winner's colour,
    letterbox 1.5 s, a shockwave.
- Produces: `pub fn wire(rec: &Recording2, colours: [[f32; 3]; 2], step_to_t: &dyn Fn(u32) -> f64, out: &mut
  FxTimeline)`. It maps events v2 to presets (Contact by strength, Block, Clash, Launch, Land, Slam, Spawn and
  Expire, Ko, and Cue(Flash) and Cue(Shake)). Fighter looks add their own records through `Look2::fx(&self, rec,
  out)`, a new trait method with an empty default.
- Produces: `bf2-render`'s frame path (E2 Task 6's `render_frame`) gets an `fx: &FxTimeline` argument and
  draws through `render_accum` with `subframes`.
