## Feedback worth knowing (not requests)

- **The TUBA is the most criticised fighter, on every platform:** IG "Ref why does flute have like 30 abilities when tuba
  has like 1" (79), "what even is the tuba ability" (15); TT "Buff tuba next time please" (6) + a long thread; an IG idea:
  "make it twice as big and deal damage in a huge area but attack slow". The SOUSAPHONE (#53) is the march-and-blast
  low brass fans may want the tuba to be.
- **Ranged vs close combat feels unfair** to some (IG, 24, on Trumpet vs Sax).
- **People want the soundtracks** as full songs (YT, 3 comments: "needs a full song", "I need an ost of this").
- Band kids love insider ability names: "power slide should have been called 6th position bro" (TT, 26).
- Section pride drives comments: trombone players flood the trombone videos (IG "TROMBONE ATE THE GOAT" 778, "Proud to
  be a trombone player" 408), horn players answer back. Pick match-ups between big sections.
